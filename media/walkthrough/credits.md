# Track against your allowance

Set `usageLogger.monthlyCreditLimit` to your Copilot Business/Enterprise premium-request allowance.

With a limit set, the dashboard and status bar show:

- a **daily budget** (remaining credits ÷ remaining days in the month),
- **remaining today**, and
- a **burn-rate forecast** — projected month-end spend vs. your limit.

Working across multiple machines? This extension only sees local activity, so run **Copilot Usage: Sync Actual Credits Used This Month** and enter the real total from the native Copilot Business flyout to reconcile.
